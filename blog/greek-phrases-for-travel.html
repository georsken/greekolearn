10 Greek Phrases to Learn Before You Travel

Travel GreekPublished 24 February 20262 min read

You do not need to be fluent to enjoy Greece or Cyprus. Ten well-chosen phrases will get you through greetings, restaurants, shops and directions, and locals will appreciate the effort.

The ten phrases

GreekPronunciationMeaning
Γεια σας / Γεια σουYAH sas / YAH sooHello (formal or plural / informal)
Καλημέρα / Καλησπέραkah-lee-MEH-rah / kah-lee-SPEH-rahGood morning / Good evening
Παρακαλώpah-rah-kah-LOHPlease, you're welcome, here you go
Ευχαριστώ πολύef-hah-ree-STOH poh-LEEThank you very much
Συγγνώμηsee-GHNOH-meeSorry, excuse me
Πόσο κάνει;POH-so KAH-neeHow much is it?
Πού είναι η τουαλέτα;poo EE-neh ee too-ah-LEH-tahWhere is the toilet?
Μιλάτε αγγλικά;mee-LAH-teh ang-lee-KAHDo you speak English?
Δεν καταλαβαίνωthen kah-tah-lah-VEH-noI don't understand
Τον λογαριασμό, παρακαλώton lo-ghah-ree-AZ-mo pah-rah-kah-LOHThe bill, please

How to use them

1. Greetings

Say Γεια σας when you enter a shop, café or taxi. It works as both "hello" and "goodbye" and is polite with strangers. Use Γεια σου with friends and people your age. From about midday, switch from Καλημέρα to Καλησπέρα. For more, see our guide to Greek greetings and politeness.

2. Παρακαλώ

This is the most useful word in Greek. It means "please", "you're welcome", "go ahead" and "here you are". If someone hands you something and says παρακαλώ, answer with ευχαριστώ.

3. Asking prices

Πόσο κάνει; works for anything you can point at. To understand the answer you will need numbers, so learn Greek numbers from 1 to 100.

4. When you are lost

Combine Συγγνώμη with Πού είναι...; and the place: Συγγνώμη, πού είναι το λιμάνι; ("Excuse me, where is the port?"). Useful words: αριστερά (left), δεξιά (right), ευθεία (straight ahead).

5. When you don't understand

Δεν καταλαβαίνω is perfectly fine. Add πιο αργά, παρακαλώ ("slower, please") and people will usually help.

Bonus: yes, no and cheers

  • Ναι (neh) means yes. Όχι (OH-hee) means no.
  • Γεια μας! (YAH mas) means "cheers!", literally "to our health".
  • Καλή όρεξη! (kah-LEE OH-rek-see) means "enjoy your meal".
Greece or Cyprus? These phrases work in both. In Cyprus you will also hear a local dialect with its own pronunciation and words, but standard Greek is understood everywhere.

Next steps

Practise these aloud until they feel automatic, then learn how to order food and drinks in Greek. If you have a trip booked, a tutor can build a personalised travel lesson for you.
