Days, Months and Telling the Time in Greek

VocabularyPublished 8 September 20262 min read

Making plans, booking tables and catching buses all depend on time words. This guide covers the days, months, seasons and clock time, with pronunciation.

Days of the week

GreekPronunciationEnglish
Δευτέραthef-TEH-raMonday
ΤρίτηTREE-teeTuesday
Τετάρτηte-TAR-teeWednesday
ΠέμπτηPEMP-teeThursday
Παρασκευήpa-ra-ske-VEEFriday
ΣάββατοSAH-va-toSaturday
Κυριακήkee-ree-a-KEESunday

Notice that Greek names mostly follow the order: Δευτέρα is “second”, Τρίτη “third”, Τετάρτη “fourth” and Πέμπτη “fifth”. Weeks are counted from Sunday as the first day. To say “on Monday”, use τη Δευτέρα.

Many Greeks drop the ending and say τον Μάρτη, τον Ιούλη and so on in casual speech.

Seasons and useful time words

  • η άνοιξη (ee A-nik-see): spring
  • το καλοκαίρι (to ka-lo-KEH-ree): summer
  • το φθινόπωρο (to fthee-NO-po-ro): autumn
  • ο χειμώνας (o hee-MO-nas): winter
  • σήμερα (SEE-me-ra): today
  • αύριο (AV-ree-o): tomorrow
  • χθες (hthes): yesterday
  • τώρα (TO-ra): now
  • αργότερα (ar-GHO-te-ra): later

Telling the time

Ask: Τι ώρα είναι; (tee O-ra EE-ne), “What time is it?” Then answer with Είναι plus the hour. For one o'clock it is Είναι μία, and for other hours Είναι δύο, τρεις, τέσσερις, πέντε...

GreekMeaning
Είναι μία.It is one o'clock.
Είναι τρεις και πέντε.It is five past three.
Είναι τρεις και τέταρτο.It is a quarter past three.
Είναι τρεις και μισή.It is half past three.
Είναι τέσσερις παρά είκοσι.It is twenty to four.
Είναι τέσσερις παρά τέταρτο.It is a quarter to four.

Use και (and) for past the hour and παρά (less) for minutes to the hour. To say “at”, use στις (στη μία for one o'clock): Τα λέμε στις οχτώ. (See you at eight.) For numbers, check our numbers guide.

To specify morning, afternoon or night, add το πρωί (in the morning), το απόγευμα (in the afternoon), το βράδυ (in the evening) or τη νύχτα (at night).

Practise: every time you look at a clock today, say the time in Greek. Then say what you will do tomorrow using αύριο and a day of the week.
