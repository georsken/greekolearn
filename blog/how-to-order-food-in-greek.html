How to Order Food and Coffee in Greek (With Menu Words)

Travel GreekPublished 5 May 20262 min read

Eating is the heart of Greek social life, which makes restaurants and cafés the perfect place to practise. These phrases cover everything from asking for a table to paying the bill.

Getting started

  • Ένα τραπέζι για δύο, παρακαλώ. A table for two, please.
  • Τον κατάλογο, παρακαλώ. The menu, please.
  • Τι προτείνετε; What do you recommend?
  • Τι έχετε σήμερα; What do you have today?

Ordering

The most useful structure is Θα ήθελα... ("I would like...") followed by the thing you want. It is polite and works everywhere.

  • Θα ήθελα μια χωριάτικη σαλάτα. I would like a Greek salad.
  • Θα ήθελα νερό, παρακαλώ. I would like some water, please.
  • Θα πάρω το ψάρι. I will take the fish.
  • Και για μένα το ίδιο. The same for me.

Greek coffee, frappé and more

When you order a traditional Greek coffee (ελληνικός καφές), you also say how sweet you want it:

  • σκέτος (SKEH-tos): no sugar
  • μέτριος (MEH-tree-os): medium sweet
  • γλυκός (ghlee-KOS): sweet

Other common orders are φραπέ (iced instant coffee, a Greek invention), freddo espresso and freddo cappuccino. For example: Έναν φραπέ μέτριο με γάλα, παρακαλώ.

Dietary needs

  • Είμαι χορτοφάγος. I am vegetarian. The word is the same for men and women.
  • Χωρίς κρέας, παρακαλώ. Without meat, please.
  • Έχω αλλεργία στους ξηρούς καρπούς. I have a nut allergy.
  • Έχει γλουτένη; Does it contain gluten?

Many Greek dishes are naturally vegetarian, especially during fasting periods, when you will find νηστίσιμα dishes on many menus.

Paying

  • Τον λογαριασμό, παρακαλώ. The bill, please.
  • Μπορώ να πληρώσω με κάρτα; Can I pay by card?
  • Ήταν υπέροχο! It was wonderful!
Tipping: it is customary to leave a small tip for good service, often by rounding up or leaving some change, but it is not strictly required. Many restaurants also bring a free dessert or fruit at the end of the meal, called κέρασμα.
