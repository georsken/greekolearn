Greek Words You Already Know: English Words from Greek

VocabularyPublished 26 June 20262 min read

You already know more Greek than you think. English borrowed thousands of words from Greek, especially in science, medicine, politics and the arts. Recognising them gives you a head start.

Everyday words with Greek roots

EnglishGreek sourceOriginal meaning

philosophyφιλοσοφία (filosofia)love of wisdom

photographφωτογραφία (fotografia)light + writing

energyενέργεια (energia)activity, operation

crisisκρίση (krisi)decision, judgement
phenomenonφαινόμενο (fenomeno)something appearing
chaosχάος (haos)empty space, disorder
dramaδράμα (drama)action, play

Greek words that look like English

Modern Greek also borrows English words, and many sound familiar when you read them: ταξί (taxi), κομπιούτερ (computer), μπάνιο (bath), σούπερ μάρκετ (supermarket) and πάρκο (park). Reading them aloud is excellent alphabet practice. Our alphabet weekend plan will get you there.

Watch out for false friends

  • τράπεζα (trapeza) means “bank”, while τραπέζι (trapezi) is a table.
  • γυμναστήριο (yimnastirio) means “gym”, but γυμνάσιο means “junior high school”.
  • μαγαζί (magazi) is a shop, not a magazine. A magazine is περιοδικό.
  • χρονιά (hronia) means “year”, and χρόνος (hronos) means both “time” and “year”.
Study tip: when you meet a new Greek word, ask yourself which English word it might be hiding in. This connection makes memorising much faster.
