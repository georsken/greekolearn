25 Basic Greek Phrases for Travel (with Pronunciation)

25 basic Greek phrases for travel with easy pronunciation: hello, thank you, how much, ordering food and more. Perfect for English speakers.

By GreekoLearn·14 October 2026·2 min read

  5. Frequently asked questions

You do not need to be fluent to enjoy Greece. A few well-chosen phrases, said with a smile, change how people respond to you. Waiters relax, shopkeepers chat, and taxi drivers start telling you their life story. Here are 25 phrases worth learning before you land.

Greetings and manners

Eating and drinking

GreekSay it likeMeaning
Ένα τραπέζι για δύοena trapezi ya dhioA table for two
Θα ήθελα...tha ithelaI would like...
Νερό, παρακαλώnero parakaloWater, please

Καλή όρεξη!kali orexiEnjoy your meal!
Στην υγειά μας!stin yia masCheers!

Pronunciation tip: the stressed syllable is marked with an accent in Greek writing. Say Ευχαριστώ as ef-khari-STO, with the stress on the last syllable.

How to use them

Start every interaction with a greeting. In Greece, walking into a shop or café and saying Καλημέρα or Καλησπέρα before anything else is simply good manners. Then use Θα ήθελα... to ask for things politely: Θα ήθελα έναν καφέ means “I would like a coffee”.

If you get stuck, Δεν καταλαβαίνω is perfectly acceptable. Most people will happily switch to English, or slow down and repeat themselves, especially when you have tried to speak Greek first.

Frequently asked questions

How do you say hello in Greek?

The most common informal greeting is Γεια σου (ya soo). Use Γεια σας (ya sas) in formal situations or with several people. Καλημέρα (kalimera) means good morning.

How do you say thank you in Greek?

Thank you is Ευχαριστώ (efkharisto). Say Ευχαριστώ πολύ (efkharisto poli) for thank you very much.

Do people in Greece speak English?

Many people do, especially in tourist areas and among younger people. Trying a few Greek phrases is always appreciated and often leads to warmer conversations.
