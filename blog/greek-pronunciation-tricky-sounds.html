Greek Pronunciation Guide for English Speakers

Greek pronunciation guide for English speakers: master γ, χ, θ, δ, ρ, vowel pairs like ου and αι, and stress, with examples and tips.

By GreekoLearn·12 October 2026·2 min read

In this article

  1. The five consonants to practise first
  2. Vowels and vowel pairs
  3. Letter pairs that make new sounds
  4. Stress changes everything
  5. Frequently asked questions

Greek is one of the friendlier languages to pronounce, because each letter or letter pair has one main sound. The challenge is that a few sounds do not exist in English. The good news: you can learn all of them in an afternoon of practice.

The five consonants to practise first

LetterSoundExample
γBefore ε, ι, η, υ it sounds like y in yes. Before other letters it is a soft, voiced gh.γεια (ya) hello, γάλα (gala) milk
χA breathy kh like Scottish loch. Softer before ε and ι, like the h in huge.χαρά (hara) joy, χέρι (heri) hand
θAlways the th in think.θέλω (thelo) I want
δAlways the th in this.δέκα (dheka) ten
ρA light tap of the tongue, a bit like Spanish r.ρόδι (rodhi) pomegranate

Vowels and vowel pairs

Greek has five vowel sounds: a, e, i, o, u. Several spellings share one sound, which is a historical quirk.

SpellingSoundExample
η, ι, υ, ει, οιee as in feetείμαι (ime) I am, οικογένεια (ikoyenia) family
αιe as in bedκαι (ke) and
εe as in bedέξι (exi) six
ο, ωo as in offόνομα (onoma) name, ώρα (ora) hour
ουoo as in moonουρανός (ouranos) sky

Stress changes everything

Every Greek word with two or more syllables has a stress mark (΄). It tells you which syllable to say louder. Always say the stress, because it makes you understood much more than perfect individual sounds. For example, πότε (pote, when) and ποτέ (pote, never) differ only in stress.

Practice routine: read ten words aloud every morning for a week. Pick words with γ, χ, θ and δ. Record yourself and compare with a native speaker. A tutor can catch habits that are very hard to hear yourself.

Frequently asked questions

How do you pronounce γ in Greek?

Before ε, ι, η and υ it sounds like the y in yes. Before other letters it is a soft, voiced gh sound made at the back of the mouth.

Why are there so many ways to write the ee sound in Greek?

Letters and pairs such as η, ι, υ, ει and οι were pronounced differently in ancient Greek and merged into one sound over time. The spelling kept the old forms.

How do I know which syllable to stress in Greek?

Every Greek word with two or more syllables has an accent mark over the stressed vowel. Say that syllable louder.
