Greek Greetings and Polite Phrases: Formal vs Informal

VocabularyPublished 12 March 20262 min read

Greek has two ways of saying "you": a friendly εσύ and a polite εσείς. Once you understand that single idea, Greek greetings become simple.

Hello and goodbye

GreekPronunciationWhen to use it
Γεια σουYAH sooInformal hello or goodbye to one person
Γεια σαςYAH sasFormal hello or goodbye, or to several people
ΓειαyahQuick, casual hello or bye among friends
ΧαίρετεHEH-re-teFormal hello, common in writing and at events
Καλημέραkah-lee-MEH-rahGood morning, used until around noon
Καλησπέραkah-lee-SPEH-rahGood afternoon or evening
Καληνύχταkah-lee-NEE-htaGood night, said only when parting for the night
Αντίοan-DEE-ohGoodbye, a little more final
Τα λέμεta LEH-meSee you, literally “we'll talk”

How are you?

The informal question is Τι κάνεις; (tee KAH-nees), and the formal one is Τι κάνετε; (tee KAH-ne-te). Typical answers:

  • Καλά, ευχαριστώ. Εσύ; Fine, thanks. And you?
  • Μια χαρά! Great! Literally "one joy".
  • Έτσι κι έτσι. So-so.
  • Δεν πάει άσχημα. Not bad.

Please, thank you, sorry

  • Παρακαλώ means please and you're welcome.
  • Ευχαριστώ means thank you. Add πολύ (a lot) to say thank you very much.
  • Σας ευχαριστώ is the formal "I thank you".
  • Συγγνώμη means sorry or excuse me.
  • Με συγχωρείτε is a more formal "excuse me".
  • Τίποτα means "it's nothing", a casual reply to thanks.

Meeting someone

  • Πώς σε λένε; What's your name? (informal)
  • Πώς σας λένε; What's your name? (formal)
  • Με λένε Μαρία. My name is Maria.
  • Χάρηκα πολύ. Nice to meet you.
  • Από πού είσαι; Where are you from?

Formal or informal: which should you use?

Use the formal εσείς with older people, officials, shop staff you do not know and anyone you want to show respect to. Use the informal εσύ with friends, family, children and people your own age in relaxed settings. Greeks are generally warm and quick to move to the informal form, and if they say Πες μου Γιάννη ("call me Yannis"), you can switch too.

Cheers and good wishes: Greeks have a phrase for almost every moment. Say Γεια μας! when you toast, Καλή όρεξη! before a meal and Χρόνια πολλά! for birthdays and name days.
