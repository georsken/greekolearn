How to Order Food and Coffee in Greek

How to order food and coffee in Greek: phrases for cafés and tavernas, Greek coffee types, menu words and tips for Greece and Cyprus.

By GreekoLearn·2 October 2026·2 min read

In this article

  1. Ordering coffee
  2. At the taverna
  3. Menu words you will see
  4. Customs worth knowing
  5. Frequently asked questions

Cafés and tavernas are the best classrooms in Greece. People linger, the waiter is patient, and the vocabulary repeats every visit. Here is how to handle a whole meal in Greek.

Ordering coffee

Start with Θα ήθελα έναν καφέ, παρακαλώ (tha ithela enan kafe parakalo), “I would like a coffee, please”. Then say how you want it:

GreekSay it likeMeaning
ελληνικός καφέςellinikos kafesGreek coffee, made in a small pot
σκέτοςsketosno sugar
μέτριοςmetriosmedium sweet
γλυκόςglikossweet
φραπέfrapeiced instant coffee, a Greek classic
φρέντο εσπρέσοfredo espresoiced espresso, shaken and topped with foam
με γάλαme galawith milk

Customs worth knowing

  • Sharing is normal. Many people order several small dishes for the table.
  • Water and bread often arrive without asking, and some places add a small cover charge.
  • Meals are slow. The bill usually only comes when you ask: Τον λογαριασμό, παρακαλώ.
  • Leaving a small tip, such as rounding up or a few euros, is common and appreciated.

Practice idea: next time you eat Greek food, read the whole menu in Greek letters first. Reading real menus is great practice for the alphabet.

Frequently asked questions

How do you order a Greek coffee?

Say Θα ήθελα έναν ελληνικό καφέ, μέτριο, παρακαλώ (tha ithela enan ellinikó kafe, metrio, parakalo): I would like a Greek coffee, medium sweet, please.

What is a frappé?

A frappé is an iced coffee made by shaking instant coffee with water and ice. It is a classic Greek summer drink.

How do you ask for the bill in Greek?

Say Τον λογαριασμό, παρακαλώ (ton logariazmo parakalo). The bill usually arrives only when you ask for it.
