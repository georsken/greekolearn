Introduction to Greek

Learn the alphabet, read real words, greet people and introduce yourself. Interactive, with audio, and free.

    What you will learn in Introduction to Greek

    1. Welcome to Greek. Where Greek is spoken and nine English words that are really Greek.
    2. The alphabet, part 1. Letters Α to Μ with names, sounds and audio.
    3. The alphabet, part 2. Letters Ν to Ω, including look-alikes and final sigma.
    4. Letter pairs and stress. ου, αι, ει, οι, μπ, ντ, γκ, τσ, stress marks and the Greek question mark.
    5. Reading practice. Greek words that look hard but sound like English, Greek place names and street signs.
    6. Greetings and politeness. Hello, thank you, sorry, and formal versus friendly Greek.
    7. Introduce yourself. My name is, I am from, the verb to be, and building your own introduction.
    8. Numbers 0 to 10. Counting, prices in euros and numbers that change gender.
    9. Final challenge. Twelve questions with a 70 percent pass mark.
