Questions about learning Greek

Are the Greek courses really free?

Yes. The beginner courses are free to read and practise. One-to-one lessons with a tutor are optional.

I have never studied Greek. Where do I start?

Start with the free course. The first lesson teaches the Greek alphabet and takes about an hour.

How does tutor matching work?

Fill in the short form with your level, goals, timezone and availability. We email you with a matched tutor and trial lesson times.

Is Greek hard for English speakers?

Greek has a new alphabet and grammar cases, but pronunciation is consistent and thousands of English words come from Greek, so many learners progress faster than expected.

Can I learn Greek just for travel?

Yes. Tell us your goal in the form and your tutor will focus on practical Greek for travel and daily life.
